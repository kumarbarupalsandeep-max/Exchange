Frequently asked questions

Are the prices live?

The markets page requests live public ticker data. Availability depends on the market provider and your network connection.

Can I buy or sell crypto?

No. Trading buttons are for interface testing only. No orders are transmitted and no real assets are handled.

Can I deposit or withdraw funds?

No. The wallet page is a placeholder. Do not send cryptocurrency to any address based on this demo.

How do I report an issue?

In a production build, add a support ticket form and secure service desk integration here.
